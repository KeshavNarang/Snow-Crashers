# 2026-11-07 depth investigation on skybeta-03 (read-only)

Read-only: hostname confirmed `skybeta-03`, lock was free and not taken (nothing written except
two snapshot PNGs in /tmp, since deleted). Stack running `overwall_nav/overwall.launch.py`.

## Scene
Drone stationary on a glossy table (VIO odom constant to 1 mm, IMU gravity = 26 deg pitch,
matches the 27 deg mount). Camera ~0.10 m above the table; a textured book box ~0.3 m ahead.

## Problem 1 (main): floor fit flips onto vertical surfaces
`stereo.fit_floor` is plain RANSAC with no orientation prior: it takes whichever plane has most
inliers. On the glossy table only ~28 % of pixels match, so the table and the box face tie
(~33-36 % inliers each). Consecutive frames alternate:
- table: pitch 28-37 deg, height 0.08-0.11 m (correct)
- box face: pitch 68-91 deg, height 0.27-0.34 m (wrong: that is the distance to the box)

Consequences in `mission.py`: in WAIT it takes the median of the last 30 landed fits as
`cam_ground`; with ~half the fits on the box this gives ~0.28 m instead of 0.10 m. That height
feeds `analyze()` every tick (floor rejection `u > -cam_height + 0.12`, `floor_free`) and the
height cap, so floor points get counted as obstacles / the wall top is biased by ~0.18 m.
`analyze` output also flips: `front 0.32 m` when the fit is wrong, `front clear` when right
(the correct floor rejects the short box as floor clutter).
Fix idea: reject fits whose pitch is not within ~10 deg of `camera_pitch_deg` (or IMU gravity)
and whose normal is not near vertical; or RANSAC only on points below the camera.

## Problem 2: depth node starves the camera/VIO pipeline
`stereo_depth` (Python SGBM) at 244 % CPU. `/oak/left/image_mono` measured 19 Hz (not 30);
cuVSLAM warns of 66-166 ms frame gaps continuously; depth images 100-430 ms old by publish
(README says ~100 ms). At 0.3 m/s, 430 ms = 0.13 m of travel.
