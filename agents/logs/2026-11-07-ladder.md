# 2026-11-07: the stepladder on the course, measured and added to the simulator

The organizers stood an open stepladder in the landing area, between the
launch corridor's barrier and the landing mat. Photos: `photos/Ladder_Front.jpg`
(from the west net), `Ladder_Side.jpg` (south, by the west net), `Ladder_Diag_One/Two.jpg`
(south-west), `Ladder_Vid.mp4` (walking south along the west net).

## The ladder

A 6 ft fiberglass stepladder: blue rails, yellow top cap, 5 flat aluminium
steps, rear rungs with a V brace at the bottom, spreader arms. It matches a
Werner 6000 series 6006 (spec: 6 ft, 22 in base, 41 1/8 in open spread, 3 1/8 in
front rails, 1 3/4 in rear rails). It stands open with its **rear legs toward the
west net** and its **steps facing east-north-east** (toward A1).

## How it was measured

No EXIF in the photos. For Side, Diag One and Diag Two, the camera pose was solved
(least squares, `solve.py`, `joint.py` here) against points with known course
coordinates: barrier panel 1's corners and its stripe (the scan,
`assets/course-scan.npz`: x -3.07..-2.12, south face y 0.29..0.36, stripe z 0.94,
bottom 0.28, top 1.75), the drone sitting on the launch mark (-2.52, 0.82), and the south
net's 5 ft rail (z 1.524). The four feet, labelled in each photo, were then fitted
as one rectangle shared by all three photos (focal length 2700-3000 px tried; 2850 used).

| | Value | Spread / check |

The model projected back into the photos (`ladder_overlay.jpg`, from `overlay.py`) lands
on the real feet, rails, steps, rear brace and top cap in both diagonal photos; the Side
photo, whose camera is the least certain, is off by a few cm.

**Uncertainty:** treat the pose as +-0.1 m and +-5 deg. It depends on the scan's
barrier, made the day before. The course "has been rebuilt differently several times",
and the ladder can be knocked between runs. Measure it with a tape on site if possible:
the rear feet to the west net, the north feet to the barrier panel.

## The mat in the same photos

Centred near **(-1.94, -1.47)**, yaw about -21 deg, **about 0.6 m square**. All three
photos agree to ~0.1 m. That is north of the sim's `PAD_AREA` (y <= -1.55) and
smaller than both the sim's mat (0.75-0.9 m) and the detector's `PadConfig.mat_size`
(0.85 m): a 0.6 m mat cut off by the image edge fails its `0.8 * mat_size`
gate. Not changed here; flagged for whoever owns the detector.

## In the simulator

`goalnav/localsim/messy_ladder.py` builds the mesh (groups `Ladder rails`, `Ladder top`,
`Ladder metal`) and places it in every irregular course (`messy_course.generate(...,
ladder=True)`; `goalrun --no-ladder` leaves it out). Placement: the measured pose plus
+-0.10 m and +-6 deg, scaled by severity x `JITTER`. It's drawn from its own random
generator, but the rocks, placed after it, retry around it on the shared generator, so a
seed's nets, launch jitter and accepted attempt can differ from the same seed without the
ladder (seeds 1 and 4 at severity 0.7 now take attempt 1). Compare with and without the
ladder over several seeds, not seed by seed. The ladder must clear every wall, stand and paver, or failing that clear
everything above the stands' 10 cm floor battens, sliding at most 0.2 m from the measured
pose. It is solid to the contact judge. The depth camera sees it as plywood. The RGB
camera sees the photos' colours: teal-blue rails close to the mat's blue, a yellow top.
The 3D viewer draws it in blue/yellow/aluminium.

## Flight-relevant notes

- The return (pad -> launch) flies over it. `_return_profile` puts the feet at
  >= 1.85 + 0.07 + 0.15 = 2.07 m over anything mapped taller than 0.7 m, so 0.36 m over its
  top, if the camera maps it.
- `config/goals.yaml` `pad.lookouts[1]` = (-2.10, -0.30) is inside the ladder's footprint
  (it was chosen when this spot was empty).
- The blue rails could match the mat detector's colour model. The floor-projection size
  gates should reject a vertical rail, but this is now exercised in the sim.

### The switch

`goalnav/localsim/messy_ladder.py`:

```python
POSES = {'original': Pose((-2.31, -0.62), 16.0, ...),   # first measurement, unchanged
         'moved':    Pose((-2.80, -2.50), 92.7, ...)}
DEFAULT_POSE = 'moved'           # the switch: 'original' puts it back
```

Per run: `python -m localsim.goalrun --ladder-pose original` (or `moved`; `--no-ladder`
leaves it out). `messy_course.generate(..., ladder_pose='original')` for scripts; the chosen
pose name is recorded in `course.json` (`ladder.pose`). A pose may push at most
`NET_TOLERANCE` (3 cm) past the nominal net, so the corner pose can't jitter through the
net (the generator then puts the net 2 cm outside the ladder, as for any piece against it).
Evals: `cd goalnav && python -m pytest localsim/test_messy_ladder.py` (10 tests).
