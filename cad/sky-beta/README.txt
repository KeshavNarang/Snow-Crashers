Sky Beta CAD, Stanford DroneHacks 2026
=====================================

sky-beta.step            The drone as assembled: frame plates, arms, 35 mm
                         standoffs, Pixhawk 6C, Jetson Orin Nano (simplified),
                         OAK-D S2, 4-in-1 ESC, motors and battery.
oak-d-camera-mount.stl   The OAK-D camera mount, a mesh, so it is not in the
                         STEP. It is already in its assembled position.

Units are millimetres, +Z is up, and the OAK-D is at the -Y end (the front).
Not modelled: propellers, wiring, and the downward VL53L1X rangefinder.

Designing a mount or bracket? The rulebook says what you may change:
re-mounting parts and printing new brackets is allowed, every auxiliary
sensor follows rule 4, and the drone is inspected again after any hardware
change. Nothing may be removed from the core kit.
