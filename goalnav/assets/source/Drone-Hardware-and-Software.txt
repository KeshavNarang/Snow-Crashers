Jetson Orin Nano
Companion computer
The drone’s high-level computer, where your code runs. It reads the camera, works out a path and decides the next movement to ask the Pixhawk for.
Takes inImages and depth from the camera; flight state from the Pixhawk.
Sends outWhere to go next, as position or velocity targets, to the Pixhawk.
The Jetson decides where to go. The Pixhawk makes the fast corrections that keep the drone stable on the way.

Programming it
Every drone arrives ready to fly. PX4 is flashed and tuned, and the Jetson has ROS 2 and the camera libraries installed. We give you the libraries and a flying starter project, so you spend the weekend on autonomy, not on toolchains.
01
Write it on the Jetson
Your code runs on the Jetson Orin Nano as ROS 2 nodes. Try it in simulation on your laptop first.
02
See with the camera
The OAK-D S2 computes depth on the camera itself. Its ROS 2 topics give you color, depth and detections.
03
Fly through PX4
Send position or velocity setpoints to PX4 in offboard mode, and read back where the drone thinks it is.
04
Start from the starter project
A flying baseline: an offboard control node, a depth-to-obstacle example, launch files and a Gazebo simulation.
